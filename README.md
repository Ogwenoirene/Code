## The problem we're solving

> "customers call to book,but we're busy cutting hair,so calls go unattended. people walk in, see five people
>waiting and leave. meanwhile at 2pm my barbers sit idle. we keep booking in paper dairy and end up with double booking every week"
>**Joseph, Owner,Great Cut Barbershop,Killimani, Nairobi**

## features

**for customers**
- Browse **services** with prices (KES) and duration, and **barbers** with their speciality.
- Click a chair on the **shop floor plan**

**for Barbershops**
- **Dashbord** with today's stats, Schedules (status badges), weekly availability and update-hours form.

**Everywhere**
- mobile- first, responsive system
- **Dark mode** toggle (remembers your choice)

3. in vs code ,right-click `index.html` and choose **Open with live server**
